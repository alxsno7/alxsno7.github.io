Bluetooth Speaker

| Dec 2024 - Mar 2025
I built a Bluetooth speaker for my highschool capstone. Since I was given a lot of freedom for this project, I wanted to make a useful and quality device that I would use day to day.
With this being one of my first complicated projects, I learned a lot about planning and managing a self-directed project. Since I had to produce a video presentation, I made sure to document my process and progress while making sure to maintain a coherent timeline. I also taught myself 3D modeling and printing, soldering, and circuit design for the internal components.
